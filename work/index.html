SimpleReport is the CDC’s free tool for clinics and testing sites to report rapid test results to public health departments. Our team expanded it beyond COVID-19 to HIV, syphilis and hepatitis C testing.

I led design for the result-reporting and facility management workflows, the screens staff use dozens of times a day. Testing for HIV meant asking sensitive follow-up questions without slowing anyone down, so I designed progressive, plain-language forms that appear only when a result requires them.

I also built the design system that keeps every flow consistent, accessible and easy to extend to new conditions.

Shake Shack is modernizing its technology as it grows, rebuilding how guests order and how teams run each Shack.

I designed across Shake Shack’s mobile app, in-store kiosk and internal operations tools, on the Technology team. My work covered guest ordering flows and the staff-facing tools that keep service running smoothly.

The mobile app mayk.it is a simple music studio on your iPhone, used by amateur artists to make songs that go viral on TikTok. Its creators have made hundreds of thousands of songs, with billions of plays across social media.

I joined shortly after they closed a $4 million seed round. I audited the app, interviewed new users and dug into analytics to find where first-time creators were dropping off, then redesigned onboarding to get people to their first song faster.
